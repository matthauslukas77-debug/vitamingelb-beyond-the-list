# How this repo is worked in

BärnHäckt 2026 · PostFinance "Beyond the List" · two people + one remote session.

## Structure is prescribed, not chosen

The jury requires exactly these four folders in **one single repository** — no second repo,
no renaming, no extra top-level folders:

```
Code/            the runnable solution
Documentation/   technical jury doc + challenger doc
Presentation/    one landscape page + video link
Misc/            mockups, additional material
```

## What lands here

This repository is the jury-facing surface. Our research, drafts, interview notes and dead ends
live in a separate local `WORKSPACE/` that is **not** pushed.

**Promotion rule:** nothing enters this repo without both of us agreeing it is jury-ready.
Half-finished material in `Misc/` costs points; it does not earn them.

## Branching

- Branch per person/feature: `kaspar/…`, `<name>/…`. Short-lived, merged back often.
- The remote session works in its own branch/worktree. **Never force-push.**
- `main` must be runnable at all times — the technical jury has access from Saturday 12:00 and
  may look at any moment.

## Freeze

**Sunday 12:00 is hands-off.** No commits to `main`/`master` after that, for anyone.

## Commits

Present tense, one concern per commit, scope prefix where useful (`feat:`, `fix:`, `docs:`).
Decisions with consequences get a line in our decision log, not just a commit message.

## Data & licensing

- No real customer data, ever. All transaction data is synthetic and generated by us.
- Secrets stay out of the repo — `.env` is gitignored, `.env.example` is not.
- **PostFinance Grotesk must not be shipped** (licensing penalty). Inter 300/500/700 instead.
